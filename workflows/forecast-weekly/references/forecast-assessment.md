# Review forecast meaning before arithmetic

For every current-quarter deal retain its ID, proposed bucket, rationale, exact
buyer quotes and full-body source paths. Record signature/decision date ranges,
source/speaker/time, open blockers with owners/dates, and contradictory statements.
Unknown timing and conflicts remain needs-input; do not infer them from CRM dates
or stage labels. A milestone is not a signature/decision date.

A buyer deferral beyond the quarter excludes the deal unless a later buyer statement
explicitly revises that timing. Retain both statements and the exact revision quote;
a newer unrelated message cannot override the deferral. Before Commit/Upside,
verify the configured timing and bucket criteria beside all contrary evidence.

Reconcile calculation IDs, populations, source amounts, currency and revenue basis
against the covered source census. Every in-scope deal appears exactly once.
Arithmetic helpers validate values and sums, not these interpretations or omitted
sources. Unknown booked/commit amounts prevent a complete call; missing target
prevents gap/path claims. Re-review changed evidence before approving exact CRM
category corrections. These are human review requirements, not an installed run engine.
